wenmeet — scheduling that shows its thinking

A when2meet-class scheduler where the AI's certainty is visible as motion: a living nebula that swirls fast when nothing is known, densifies with every response, and settles into a slow gold breath when the meeting locks. Deterministic under the hood, honest on the surface.

1 · The one principle

Motion is uncertainty made visible. The nebula is a certainty gauge, never a mascot. Open meeting → fast chaotic swirl (teal). Each acceptance adds mass and calm. Locked → slow breathing (gold). Conflict → amber flicker until re-locked. Every state the backend can be in has exactly one motion signature, so users read system state without reading text.

OPEN→ COMPUTING→ PROPOSED→ LOCKED⇄ DESTABILIZED / RECOMPUTING

3 · Success metrics & non-goals

  • Lock quality (primary): a lock counts as successful only if all tagged decision-makers attend and, for meetings with >3 invitees, ≥50% of invitees attend.
  • Quorum is a soft rule: scoring heavily weights DM presence + attendance ratio, but the AI may propose below-quorum with an explicit warning chip.
  • Secondary: time-to-lock < 24h median; invitee response rate ≥80%; zero silent reschedules (every change is announced with history).
  • Non-goals v1: being a video-call product (blobs are v2 and the media layer is deferred), replacing calendars (we write to Google, we don't render a calendar app), chat/LLM personality (the AI writes nothing free-form — see scoring).
